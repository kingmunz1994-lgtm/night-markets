# Night Markets — 90-Day Pilot Plan
**Nigeria & Kenya · April–June 2026**

---

## Overview

Launch Night Markets in two high-unbanked, mobile-first markets where Midnight's existing node operators (MoneyGram, Worldpay) already have local payment rails. Target 1,000 active sellers and 10,000 buyers in 90 days. Prove the "everyone earns" flywheel generates real GMV and measurable DUST consumption on mainnet.

---

## Target Market

| Metric | Nigeria | Kenya |
|---|---|---|
| Unbanked population | 60M+ | 15M+ |
| Mobile penetration | 85% | 90% |
| Mobile money users | 40M+ | 35M+ |
| Existing crypto activity | High (top 3 globally) | High (M-Pesa integration ready) |
| Midnight partners active | MoneyGram | MoneyGram + Worldpay |

---

## Month 1 — Onboarding & Infrastructure

**Week 1–2: Partner onboarding**
- Activate fiat on/off-ramp with MoneyGram Nigeria + Kenya APIs
- Sign MOU with 3 artisan cooperatives (Lagos Artisans Guild, Nairobi Makers Collective, one TBD Manila co-op for Phase 2 preview)
- Deploy Night Markets to Midnight mainnet (upgrade from Preprod contract)
- Set up USSD discovery flow for feature phones

**Week 3–4: Seller onboarding (target: 500 sellers)**
- Onboarding sessions with artisan co-ops (in-person + WhatsApp group)
- Each seller: creates mini-store + lists 3–5 products
- First 50 sellers offered "Founding Business Token" status: free launch fee, boosted staking yield for 60 days
- Referral program activated: invite a seller → both get 500 NIGHT bonus

**Key deliverable:** 500 sellers live, at least 10 Business Tokens launched with payback % set

---

## Month 2 — GMV Ramp

**Target: $200k GMV, 5,000 active buyers**

**Buyer acquisition:**
- Social commerce push: TikTok/Instagram short videos of sellers using the app
- Diaspora channel: target Nigeria/Kenya diaspora in AU/UK/US as buyers (remittance angle)
- MoneyGram partner push: existing MoneyGram users in-app notification about Night Markets
- ZK-BNPL as hook: "Buy now, 4 payments, no credit check" messaging

**Seller activation:**
- Weekly "dividend day" events — push notification when dividends land
- Top 10 sellers featured on Night Markets homepage
- First 500 NIGHT cashback for buyers who spend 1,000+ NIGHT

**Key deliverable:** First dividend payouts distributed via smart contract — on-chain verifiable

---

## Month 3 — Tokens, Staking & Retention

**Target: $500k cumulative GMV, 50 Business Tokens live, 60% week-4 retention**

**Token economy activation:**
- Run "Invest in your local market" campaign — buyers purchase Business Tokens from sellers they shop with
- First invoice token launch: help 3 sellers tokenize outstanding invoices
- Community co-op token: Lagos Artisans Guild launches collective token (group of 20 sellers, one pooled dividend)

**Staking push:**
- "Savings Account" feature highlight: show users their projected annual yield
- Family plan: shared staking pool boosts for household groups
- Gamified dashboard: streaks, badges, "your money today" real-time display

**Bitcoin whale activation (concurrent):**
- Post BTC on-ramp feature to r/Bitcoin, relevant X crypto accounts
- Offer first 100 BTC on-ramp users zero conversion fee + priority OTC desk access

## Risk Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| NIGHT price volatility | Auto-convert option for sellers (instant fiat via MoneyGram at checkout) |
| Regulatory scrutiny on tokens | Tiered ZK-KYC: low-value = private; >$500 = simple ZK eligibility proof |
| Low-connectivity users | USSD discovery + QR claim flow + offline queue for proof submission |
| Dispute fraud | Escrow + 48hr dispute window + privacy-preserving mediation first |
| Smart contract bugs | Full audit before mainnet deploy + bug bounty (paid in NIGHT) |
| Slow seller onboarding | In-person co-op sessions + WhatsApp support group in Swahili/Pidgin |
