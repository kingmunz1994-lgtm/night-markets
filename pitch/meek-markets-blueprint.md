## What is Meek Markets?

Meek Markets is the **licensable blueprint** for private, community-owned commerce on any blockchain.

Night Markets is the first implementation (Midnight Network). ADA Markets is the second (Cardano). The blueprint is the same every time:

1. **Private marketplace** — buy and sell without surveillance
2. **Community Chests** — every token community earns from its own activity only
3. **Tax-free borrowing** — borrow against holdings, never trigger a CGT event
4. **The Playbook** — 12 wealth strategies, explained for that chain's users
5. **Business Token launchpad** — any seller tokenizes their revenue stream
6. **Night ID bridge** — KYC once, valid everywhere

The model travels. The identity travels. The wealth strategies travel. Only the underlying chain changes.

## The standard components

### 1. Private Marketplace (Shop)
- Escrow-backed listings using that chain's smart contract standard
- Buyer identity, purchase history, and amounts are never on-chain
- ZK checkout proof: payment confirmed without revealing who paid
- Cashback in the native governance token on every purchase

### 2. Community Chests
- Each community token (SNEK, HOSKY, DOGE, any CNT/SPL/BRC-20) gets a ring-fenced chest
- Revenue from that community's activity only flows into their chest
- Holders of the OG token stake to earn from their community — no token swap required
- New communities apply by holding the minimum threshold + community vote
- **The moat:** Cardano has 1,300+ CNTs. Solana has 100,000+ SPL tokens. Every one is a potential chest.

### 3. Tax-Free Borrowing (Lend)
- Lock native token as collateral at 60% LTV
- Borrow local stablecoins (iUSD, DJED, USDC, sUSD depending on chain)
- No sell event = no capital gains tax event
- Interest rate: 4–8% APR depending on collateral quality
- **The pitch to every holder:** Your ADA/SOL/BTC keeps compounding. You use the cash. The tax man waits forever.

### 4. The Playbook (12 wealth strategies)
The same 12 strategies, localised for each chain's holder base:
1. Borrow don't sell — avoid CGT
2. Hold 12 months — CGT discount
3. Stake overnight — earn while you sleep
4. Community Chests — earn from your tribe
5. BNPL cash flow — buy now, cashback compounds
6. Cut Stripe/SWIFT fees — Enterprise package
7. Tokenize your income — Business Tokens
8. Own your data — Night ID / Night Data
9. Lend your stablecoins — earn 6–9% APR
10. SMSF play (AU) — 15% vs 45% tax
11. Remittance saving — replace 6.49% fees
12. Compound small — start now, not later

### 5. Business Token Launchpad
- Any seller creates a revenue-sharing token
- ZK proof of dividend: holders receive payback from every sale, provably
- Four token types: Merch Tokens, Invoice Tokens, Co-op Tokens, Franchise Tokens
- Works for: market stall, ecommerce brand, professional service firm, DAO

### 6. Night ID Bridge
- KYC credential issued on Midnight, bridged to any Meek Markets implementation
- ZK proof of credential — institution gets boolean answers, never raw data
- Valid forever — no re-verification across chains
- **The moat:** Identity is the hardest problem. We solve it once. Every new chain implementation inherits it.

## Why this model wins everywhere

### Network effects multiply across chains
A seller on Night Markets who tokenizes their revenue stream already has a Business Token. When ADA Markets launches, they get a second market for their token. When SOL Markets launches, a third. The token doesn't move — the buyers come to it.

### Community identity is the moat
SNEK holders are SNEK holders on every chain they use. The SNEK Community Chest on Night Markets and on ADA Markets both funnel back to the same community. The cultural identity travels with the chest model.

### Night ID eliminates the identity race
Every other chain-specific marketplace will need to solve KYC separately — expensive, slow, legally complex. We solve it once on Midnight with the gold standard (MoneyGram + Monument Bank as issuers). Every new chain implementation inherits a working identity layer from day one.

### Speed is the only real moat right now
The Midnight mainnet launched March 30, 2026. The Cardano ecosystem is waiting for a consumer application. The 6-12 month window before well-funded teams copy this model is the entire strategy. Build fast. Get users. Let the community lock-in do the rest.

## The licensing play

Once Night Markets and ADA Markets are live with real GMV, the Meek Markets blueprint becomes a licensable standard:

- **Chain foundations** can license the blueprint and fund an implementation (Solana Foundation, Bitcoin ecosystem funds)
- **Existing platforms** (eBay, Shopify, Etsy) can integrate specific components (Community Chests, Business Tokens, Night ID)
- **Regional operators** can white-label a regional implementation (e.g. "Lagos Markets" on Midnight, "Delhi Markets" on Cardano)

The license fee is simple: 10% of GMV fee revenue to Meek Markets Pty Ltd. The licensee keeps 90%. We build the standard once. They operate it locally.
