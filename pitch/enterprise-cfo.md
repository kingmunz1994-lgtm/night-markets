## How it works in plain English

Midnight Network has a two-token model:
- **NIGHT** — the capital token. You buy it once and hold it.
- **DUST** — the fee token. It generates automatically from your NIGHT stake, continuously, forever.

Every transaction on Midnight costs DUST. Your company's NIGHT stake generates enough DUST to cover all your transactions. **The more NIGHT you hold, the more DUST you generate, the more transactions you can run for free.**

No monthly subscription. No per-transaction fee. No hidden costs. One purchase. Permanent benefit.

## Privacy — the thing Stripe can never offer

Every transaction on Midnight is ZK-private by default:
- Stripe sees every transaction you process
- PayPal builds profiles on your customers
- Banks report cross-border transfers to regulators automatically

**Midnight sees nothing.** Transfers are proven correct via ZK proofs — cryptographic mathematics, not trusted third parties. Your customer data stays off-chain. Your transaction history is yours.

For compliance: regulators get ZK proofs (mathematical verification without raw data exposure). GDPR, HIPAA, AML — all provable, all private.
