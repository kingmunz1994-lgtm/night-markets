## Why Midnight

Midnight is backed by IOHK (the Cardano team). It solves the transparency problem that prevents DeFi from going mainstream: businesses and individuals don't want competitors, employers, or governments seeing every transaction. Midnight uses ZK circuits so you can prove things (balance > X, reputation > Y, credential type Z) **without revealing the underlying data**.

Mainnet is expected mid-2026. We are positioned as the first full-featured dApp ecosystem on the network.
